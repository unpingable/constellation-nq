# CROWNED-STORE campaign boundary

CROWNED-STORE is the fresh local-VM authority-lifecycle successor after
GROUP-ANCHOR. It may use only a fresh campaign-owned VM and the exact
reproducible GROUP-ANCHOR release. CALIPER remains terminal and consumed.

Entry requires exact source/package/base-image digests, absent predecessor
state, inert package installation, exact host identity, observer/provider
identity access, a real pristine generation store with mode exactly `0750` or
`02750`, and the minimum free-space floor. Any mismatch stops before authority.

After authority, every failure is terminal. No transition may be patched
through, retried, or replayed. A new campaign and fresh occurrence are required.
